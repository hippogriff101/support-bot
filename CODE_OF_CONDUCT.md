# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be kind, assume good faith, welcome newcomers, and keep
criticism about the work, not the person. Harassment, discrimination and
personal attacks aren't tolerated in issues, pull requests, reviews, or
anywhere else in the project.

Many contributors are teenagers and Hack Clubbers. Please keep that in
mind, and see Hack Club's [Code of Conduct](https://hackclub.com/conduct/)
too.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, report it privately
through the repository's **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability**
(use it for conduct reports too), or contact a maintainer directly on
GitHub. Reports are kept confidential, and maintainers will respond and
act as the Contributor Covenant describes.
